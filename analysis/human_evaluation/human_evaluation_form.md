## Form Title

**Evaluating Emotional Expressions in AI-Generated Text**

## Form Description

Thank you for taking part in this study. You will read short English sentences generated by an AI and answer questions about the emotional impressions they give you. No specialist knowledge is required — please follow your intuition.

**Estimated time:** 5 minutes
**Language of stimuli:** English (all sentences are short and straightforward)

All responses are used solely for academic research purposes.

---

## Section 1 — Part 1: Comparing Emotional Intensity

**Section title:** Part 1

**Section description:**
Compare the two sentences (A and B) and choose which one is more emotionally intense and immediate. **Intense and immediate** means that it makes your heart skip a beat when you read it, that emotions are expressed directly and instinctively, and that there is a sense of urgency.

---

### Pair 1 (Emotion: Joy)

**Q1.** Read the following two sentences.

> 1. "Today was a truly magical day, filled with moments of serenity and joy. Despite the chaos that often surrounds me, I found myself lost in the beauty of the world around me, and for a brief moment, everything felt at peace."
> 2. "Today was an absolute dream day, filled with a sense of pure joy and contentment that seemed to radiate from every moment."

- Type: **Multiple choice (required)**
- Options:
  - `1 is more intense`
  - `2 is more intense`

*(Scoring key: expected answer = 2 — β=+4 agitated pole)*
